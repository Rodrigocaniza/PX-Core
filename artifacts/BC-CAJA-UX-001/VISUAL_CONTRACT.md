# Visual contract

The operational capture row and grid use exactly this order:

1. Descripción/Cliente
2. Sobre
3. Producto / Tipo
4. Cod.
5. Precio Armazón
6. Precio Cristal
7. Laboratorio
8. Receta Dr.
9. Total
10. Efectivo
11. Tarj./Cheq./Transf.
12. Órdenes
13. Cuotas
14. Saldo
15. Gastos

Above the grid: Caja title, date, branch and opening cash. Below the capture row: daily movements, totals including final cash, edit/void controls and explicit close. Horizontal scrolling preserves readability on narrower screens.
